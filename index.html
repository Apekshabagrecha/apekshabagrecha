Get to Know Me

A passionate engineer who thrives at the intersection of software and hardware.

I'm a Software Engineer at MathWorks, currently contributing to the Instrument Control Toolbox (ICT) team. My work involves building MATLAB apps and enabling seamless communication with hardware instruments using protocols such as UDP, TCP/IP, and VISA.

I develop solutions using C++, MATLAB, and industry-standard communication protocols to help engineers and researchers interact effectively with their lab and industrial hardware. My technical toolkit includes strong proficiency in object-oriented programming, design patterns, and SOLID principles — skills I use daily to write clean, scalable, and maintainable software.

Beyond coding, I'm deeply interested in leadership and team-building. I aspire to take on greater responsibilities that allow me to mentor others, influence product direction, and drive collaborative success. I enjoy working in cross-functional teams and believe that clear communication and accountability are key to delivering impactful results.
